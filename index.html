The Renaissance

Seven historic and impactful events zoomed through

1400

The Beginning

Beginning of the 14th century period in European history a cultural, artistic, and political rebirth. Some of the greats thinkers authors artists and scientists were born during this era which led to global exploration of philosophy, art, and literature.

1453

The Fall of Constantinople

On May 29, 1453 the end of the Byzantine empire was underway. The proceeding centuries of conflict between the Greeks and the Christians was now concluded. Ottoman Empire finally managed to capture the Byzantine empire's capital after 53 grueling days.

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_Constantinople

1492

Columbus's Journey to the Americas

Christopher Columbus set Sail in 1492 with the hopes of finding a route to Asia, however instead of going directly to Asia he accidentally detoured into the Americas. The historic event which opened the door for European conquest in the Westen world.

Source: https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/history/christopher-columbus-americas

1504

Michelangelo Creates David

Michelangelo created David, a marble Renaissance sculpture that defined an artistic precedent for the 16th century and beyond. The biblical figure David was a symbolic reference to the civil liberites in the 1494 constitution of the Rublic of Florenece.

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_(Michelangelo)

1513

Machiavelli Writes The Prince

Makaveli transformed western political ideology by doing something never before done. Separating politics from Christian ethics. This brought in the idea of political realism and secularization of power.

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Prince

c. 1600

The End of the Renaissance

Around the 17th century the Renaissance what is thought to be concluded when in 1527 Rome was sacked by the holy Roman emperor Charles V. Not only this but the previous Italian wars cause decades of devastated local economies. The end of the Renaissance brought us to the new era's where artistic political and literature ideologies had been changed forever.
